# RB-008 Alert storm and duplicate alerts (version 1, current)

## Supported incident types
any; many repeated alerts

## Preconditions
The same alert repeats many times or comes from several monitors for one service.

## Investigation steps
1. Group alerts that share a service and fall within 10 minutes into one incident.
2. Keep every source alert as evidence; do not delete duplicates.
3. Find the underlying metric and open the matching runbook, for example RB-002 for CPU.
4. Take one action for the incident, not one per alert.

## Approved remediation
No action by itself. Correlate first, then follow the runbook for the underlying problem.

## Required permission
Not applicable

## Risks and side effects
Acting once per alert can cause repeated restarts or scaling.

## Rollback procedure
Not applicable.

## Verification criteria
Alert volume stops after the underlying problem is fixed.

## Escalation
If alerts continue after recovery, review the monitor thresholds with the owner.
