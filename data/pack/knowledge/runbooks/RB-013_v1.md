# RB-013 Flapping and noisy alerts (version 1, current)

## Supported incident types
latency; cpu; memory

## Preconditions
The metric crosses the threshold for 1 or 2 minutes at a time and returns to normal on its own.

## Investigation steps
1. Check how long each spike lasts and whether it repeats on a schedule.
2. Look for a scheduled job at the same times.
3. Confirm that errors and availability are unaffected.

## Approved remediation
No action. Observe for 15 minutes and recommend a longer sustain period on the alert threshold.

## Required permission
Not applicable

## Risks and side effects
Acting on a self-resolving blip causes unnecessary change.

## Rollback procedure
Not applicable.

## Verification criteria
No customer impact and the metric stays normal.

## Escalation
If spikes grow longer or add errors, treat as a real incident and open RB-004.
