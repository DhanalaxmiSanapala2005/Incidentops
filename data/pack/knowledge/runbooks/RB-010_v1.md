# RB-010 TLS certificate or secret expiry (version 1, current)

## Supported incident types
downtime; error_rate

## Preconditions
Logs show certificate expired, handshake failure or authentication failure after a secret rotation.

## Investigation steps
1. Confirm the expiry date in the log message or certificate details.
2. Check that a restart did not help.
3. Identify the owner of the certificate or secret.

## Approved remediation
Rotating certificates and secrets is not a sandbox operation. An administrator rotates it through the secrets process.

## Required permission
Administrator only

## Risks and side effects
Wrong rotation can break other services that share the secret.

## Rollback procedure
Restore the previous secret version from the vault if allowed.

## Verification criteria
Handshakes succeed and health check is healthy.

## Escalation
Escalate to the platform security team.
