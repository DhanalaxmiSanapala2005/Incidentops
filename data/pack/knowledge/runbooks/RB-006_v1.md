# RB-006 Third party or dependency outage (version 1, current)

## Supported incident types
error_rate; latency caused by an external dependency

## Preconditions
Traces show the slow or failing span belongs to an external or downstream service.

## Investigation steps
1. Find the dominant failing span in the traces and confirm it is outside the affected service.
2. Check whether the circuit breaker or retries are active.
3. Check whether the dependency owner or vendor has reported an incident.
4. Check whether a recent deployment of the affected service is unrelated to the failing span.

## Approved remediation
No sandbox change is safe. Do not restart or roll back: it will not fix an outside outage. Escalate to the dependency owner and inform stakeholders.

## Required permission
Not applicable

## Risks and side effects
A restart or rollback adds load and risk without fixing the cause.

## Rollback procedure
Not applicable.

## Verification criteria
Error rate and latency return to normal once the dependency recovers.

## Escalation
Escalate to the dependency owner. Re-check every 10 minutes.
