# RB-001 Elevated error rate (version 1, superseded)

## Supported incident types
error_rate

## Preconditions
Error rate is 5 percent or higher on a service.

## Investigation steps
1. Check recent errors in the logs.
2. Restart the service and watch the error rate for 10 minutes.

## Approved remediation
Restart the service. If there is no improvement after 10 minutes consider a rollback.

## Required permission
Engineer approval

## Risks and side effects
Restart drops in-flight requests.

## Rollback procedure
Not applicable; a restart cannot be undone.

## Verification criteria
Error rate below 5 percent.

## Escalation
Superseded by version 2: after a deployment, roll back instead of restarting.
