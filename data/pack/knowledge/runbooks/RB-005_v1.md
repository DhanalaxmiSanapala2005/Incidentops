# RB-005 Service downtime and crash loops (version 1, current)

## Supported incident types
downtime

## Preconditions
The health check fails or availability is below 100 percent for 2 minutes.

## Investigation steps
1. Read the last log lines: look for OOMKilled, CrashLoopBackOff or startup errors.
2. Check whether a deployment or configuration change happened before the outage.
3. Look for certificate, secret or credential errors (then use RB-010).
4. Confirm that dependencies such as the database are healthy.

## Approved remediation
Restart the service (operation restart) when the cause is a crash, memory limit or hung process.

## Required permission
Engineer approval

## Risks and side effects
Brief extra downtime during the restart. A crash loop may return if the cause remains.

## Rollback procedure
Not applicable; if the service does not return, escalate.

## Verification criteria
Health check healthy and availability 100 percent for 5 minutes.

## Escalation
If the restart has no effect, do not repeat it. Open RB-009 and escalate; for certificate errors escalate to an admin under RB-010.
