# RB-011 Monitoring or telemetry outage (version 1, current)

## Supported incident types
any; evidence sources unavailable

## Preconditions
Logs, traces or metrics requests fail or return gaps.

## Investigation steps
1. Record exactly which sources are unavailable.
2. Use the remaining sources, for example metrics only, and state the limits of the analysis.
3. Do not invent a root cause when logs and traces are missing.

## Approved remediation
No change to the service. Report limited evidence and escalate to the observability team.

## Required permission
Not applicable

## Risks and side effects
Acting on partial evidence may cause the wrong change.

## Rollback procedure
Not applicable.

## Verification criteria
Telemetry sources respond again.

## Escalation
Escalate to the observability team and keep the incident open.
